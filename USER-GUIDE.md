# Bloom User Guide

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## What Bloom is

Bloom is a private, invite-only puzzle app for a phone or tablet. Each date has one short page of three puzzles. The page begins with a softly blurred preview of the day’s artwork. Finish all three puzzles to uncover the picture and add it to your gallery.

Bloom keeps the pace relaxed. There is no countdown, hints carry no penalty, and taking a day away does not remove anything you have collected.

## Getting in

You need an invitation for the Google email address you want to use. Bloom may open directly if your browser has already confirmed that account. If a sign-in page appears:

1. Open Bloom while you are connected to the internet.
2. Tap **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose the same Google account that was invited.
4. If Bloom says it is invite-only, check that you chose the right account. If the message remains, ask the owner to add that exact email address.

Bloom normally keeps you signed in. To change accounts, open **Settings**, tap **Sign out**, and sign in again.

Your account remembers:

- how far you have reached in today’s puzzles;
- finished days and revealed artwork;
- favourites and Library keepsakes;
- your next puzzle in each Library type;
- puzzle depth; and
- display, sound, and vibration choices.

When an internet connection is available, this saved state follows the invited account between devices. Each account has its own progress and gallery.

## Finding your way around

The four tabs stay at the bottom of the screen:

- **Today** opens the current three-puzzle page.
- **More** opens the full puzzle Library.
- **Gallery** opens **Your garden**, where finished artwork and keepsakes collect.
- **Settings** holds comfort choices, puzzle depth, and account controls.

## Playing today’s page

1. Open **Today** and tap **Play today**. If you stopped partway through, tap **Continue** to resume at the next unfinished puzzle.
2. Read **How this works** and the example above the puzzle.
3. Choose **Gentle**, **Standard**, or **Deep** if you want to change the puzzle depth.
4. Play the puzzle. Tap **Hint** whenever you want help. Later hints may outline or fill one useful step.
5. When the puzzle is solved, tap **Continue**. After the third puzzle, tap **Finish** to reveal the artwork.

The first puzzle rotates between **Patterns**, **Word play**, **Odd one out**, and **Word search**. The middle puzzle is **Garden logic**. The final puzzle alternates between **Picture pieces** and **Mirror patterns**.

After finishing, you can choose **More like this**, **Something different**, or **Finish here**. You can also open the artwork in the gallery or play the daily set again just for fun. Replaying does not add a duplicate gallery picture.

## Every puzzle type

The **More** tab contains every puzzle type. Here is how each one works, in the order shown in Bloom:

- **Word search** — Find every word in the list. Drag in a straight line from the first letter to the last, or tap those two end letters. Words can run forwards or backwards.
- **Art tile matching** — All pictures stay visible. Tap one tile and then the identical tile to clear the pair. **Shuffle remaining** rearranges only the tiles still in play.
- **Letter-bank anagrams** — Read the clue, then tap letters to build its answer from left to right. Tap a filled letter box to return that letter to the bank.
- **Word ladders** — Begin with the shown word. For each clue, choose the next word that changes exactly one letter. Continue until the ladder is complete.
- **Crossword-lite** — Tap either clue, then tap its answer in the word bank. Fill both answers so they meet at their shared letter.
- **Mini-sudoku** — Select an empty square and tap a number. Each number must appear once in every row, column, and outlined region. **Candidate marks** adds small possible numbers, **Show contradictions** points out entries that do not fit, and **Clear** empties the selected square.
- **Spot the difference** — Compare the two pictures and tap each added shape in the right-hand picture.
- **Art memory pairs** — Turn over two cards and find companions that belong together. They may be related pictures rather than identical ones. Matched pairs remain open, and **Preview positions** lets you look at the whole board whenever you like.
- **Tiny Picross** — Use the numbers beside each row and column. Each number tells you the length of a run of filled squares. Tap a square once to fill it, again to mark it empty, and once more to clear it.
- **Gentle shape-fit** — Tap a coloured piece and then its matching silhouette, or drag the piece into place. You can hide or show the silhouettes.
- **Quiet connections** — Tap a filled starting dot. Continue by tapping one neighbouring square at a time—up, down, left, or right—until the route reaches its matching ring. Complete every coloured route.
- **Word play** — Read the clue and unscramble the letters. Tap letters to fill the answer, and tap a filled box to return a letter.
- **Patterns** — Look for the one feature that changes: colour, number, turn, or size. Tap the tile that continues the rule.
- **Garden logic** — Use the clues to match each gardener with one flower and one pot colour. Tap a grid square to cycle from blank to ✓, then ✗, then blank.
- **Picture pieces** — Use the small complete picture as a reference. Tap two tiles to swap them until the 3 × 3 picture is restored.
- **Odd one out** — Five tiles match. Tap the single tile whose colour, shape, or number of dots is different.
- **Mirror patterns** — The left half is already filled. Tap squares on the right until they mirror the left side. Tap a filled square again to clear it.

Wrong choices do not take anything away. Every puzzle has hints, and some puzzle boards use a soft outline to show the next helpful place.

## More puzzles and keepsakes

Open **More** whenever you want a puzzle outside the daily page. Choose a type, finish it, and tap **Another puzzle** for the next one of that type. You can stop whenever you like.

Library play adds cumulative keepsakes to **Your garden**. These record the kinds of puzzles you have explored and do not expire. Library puzzles do not reveal the blurred artwork on **Today**; that picture is added only when the three daily puzzles are finished.

## The artwork reveal and Your garden

Finishing the daily page uncovers that date’s artwork and adds it to **Gallery**. The gallery shows:

- the total number of days bloomed;
- milestone badges that unlock as the total grows;
- Library keepsakes;
- favourite pictures at the top; and
- all revealed pictures in monthly folios, newest first.

Tap the heart on a picture to add or remove it from **Favourites**. Tap the downward arrow to save a picture. On a supported phone or tablet, Bloom opens the share sheet; on other devices, it saves an image file. The completion page also has a **Save image** button.

Missing a date does not reduce the total or remove a badge, keepsake, favourite, or picture.

## Comfort and display settings

Open **Settings** to change:

- **Calm colour palette** — uses Bloom’s quietest colour balance. It starts on.
- **Calm mode (reduced motion)** — removes animation and movement.
- **Dark theme** — changes Bloom to a darker colour scheme.
- **Higher contrast** — strengthens text, outlines, and dividing lines.
- **Larger text** — increases the size of text throughout Bloom.
- **Soft completion sound** — plays a quiet chime when a puzzle is finished. It starts on and can be turned off.
- **Gentle haptic tap** — gives a short vibration when a puzzle is finished on phones that support it. It starts on and can be turned off.

Before you set these choices yourself, Bloom follows the device’s dark-theme, reduced-motion, and higher-contrast preferences.

**Puzzle depth** has three choices: **Gentle**, **Standard**, and **Deep**. In puzzle types that use depth, it changes such things as the board size, word length, or number of pairs and steps. You can change depth in **Settings** or while a puzzle is open.

## Using Bloom offline

Open Bloom online at least once so the app can save the pieces it needs on the device. After that, the app and its puzzles can open without an internet connection, and the device keeps a local copy of progress and the gallery.

Signing in and sharing saved state between devices need an internet connection. If you plan to be offline, use the same device and browser where Bloom has already opened successfully.

## Adding Bloom to the Home Screen

On an iPhone or iPad:

1. Open Bloom in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**. Depending on the Safari layout, you may first need to tap **More**, then **Share**.
3. Scroll to **Add to Home Screen**.
4. If **Open as Web App** appears, turn it on, then tap **Add**.

On an Android phone:

1. Open Bloom in Chrome.
2. Tap **More** beside the address bar.
3. Tap **Add to home screen** or **Install app**, then tap **Install**.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions.

You can then open Bloom from its flower icon like any other app.

## If something looks wrong or out of date

A saved copy can occasionally be older than the current version. Close Bloom completely and reopen it, then close and reopen it a second time. If Bloom is on the Home Screen, swipe it away between each opening. Those two full reopenings allow the stale saved version to be replaced.

If sign-in fails, reconnect to the internet and check that the invited Google account is selected. If a problem remains, tell the owner which tab and puzzle were open and what appeared on the screen.
